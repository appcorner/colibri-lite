## Measurement limitation

Passing ADR 0065 with nonzero correlated physical I/O means the gate observed
at least one correlated physical disk-read event during the workload. It does
not mean the entire 679,477,248-byte artifact was read from disk. The
correlated byte count of 1,048,576 is a small fraction of the artifact size.

This limitation applies equally to all three group-32 runs. The gate was not
adjusted or reinterpreted after observing the results. The limitation must be
carried into the final R1.1a candidate review.
