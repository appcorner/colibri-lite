## Direct-consumption boundary

R1.2 continued to use `R1_1CandidateReader` and the direct packed group-32 Layer-0 path. The test asserted peak packed expert storage of 5,308,416 bytes, nonzero candidate payload reads, and no complete F32 candidate weight materialization. Router, norms, attention, Layers 1-47, KV semantics, final norm, and LM head remained F32/reference behavior.

## Decision

R1.2 closes `GO`. R1.3 may begin paired release-process F32/candidate measurements for TTFT, throughput, working set, private bytes, logical/physical I/O, bytes/token, and cache metrics. This result does not authorize M6.4 or an all-layer candidate rollout.
