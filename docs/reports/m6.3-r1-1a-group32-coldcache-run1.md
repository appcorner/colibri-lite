## Measurement limitation

ADR 0065 requires at least one correlated candidate disk-read byte. Run 1
observed 1,048,576 bytes and therefore passes the gate as pre-registered.

This does not establish that the complete 679,477,248-byte artifact was
physically read from the device. The observed correlated total is a small
fraction of the file, so most of the 842 logical reads may still have been
served from the filesystem cache. The gate proves that candidate-correlated
physical I/O was observable and non-zero, which is exactly what it was written
to prove after earlier warm-cache attempts returned `not_measured`.

This limitation is recorded as-is. The gate is not reinterpreted, strengthened,
or weakened after seeing results, and it must be carried into the final R1.1a
review so that any conclusion about cold-start I/O cost is stated against what
was actually measured.
