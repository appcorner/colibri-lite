## Paired results

| Fixture | View | Candidate slower | Median candidate delta |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| short_english | compute-only | 5/5 | +77.35% |
| short_thai | compute-only | 5/5 | +85.79% |
| short_english | load+compute | 0/5 | -88.42% |
| short_thai | load+compute | 0/5 | -90.39% |

Candidate packed gate + up + down projections consume median 91.44% of
compute-only expert time for English and 92.04% for Thai. Routing/accumulation
share is about 0.046% for both fixtures. In load+compute, candidate load/decode
share is only 21.54% English and 23.16% Thai.

This separates the two effects cleanly: group32 materially reduces the
load-plus path, but the current safe-Rust scalar packed projection compute is
substantially slower than the F32 compute path once payloads are preloaded.
## Validation corrections

The benchmark completed all 40 samples before classification. The original
validator then failed on two schema assumptions that were not pre-registered
measurement gates:

1. ADR 0084 corrected Git commit validation from SHA-256-only to valid 40/64
   hex Git object IDs.
2. ADR 0085 allowed a measured no-op timer median of zero, preserving the exact
   `20 * noop_median` rule without applying a floor or subtraction.

Both corrections were pre-registered before their respective validator edits.
No localization sample was rerun, replaced, or modified.

## Next boundary

R2.1 may now design a vertical-slice compute hypothesis around the packed

retaining Rust routing, placement, cache policy, and correctness guards.
Implementation is not yet authorized. M6.4 remains blocked.
