## Common passing gates

Both candidates, across all three valid runs:

- preserved exact Layer-0 router IDs;
- passed fixed-logit max abs <= 0.05;
- reported first divergence at `layer0.selected_expert_output`;
- directly consumed packed values/scales;
- recorded zero complete F32 weight materializations;
- produced deterministic checkpoint/final-logit hashes within each candidate;
- passed the ADR 0065 physical-I/O observability gate with nonzero correlated bytes and zero lost events;
- remained within the measured process working-set/private-byte envelope.

## Physical-I/O limitation

The observed correlated disk-read bytes were small relative to each candidate artifact. These runs prove that process/path-correlated physical I/O was measurable and nonzero. They do not prove complete cold-device artifact reads, and no such claim is made.
