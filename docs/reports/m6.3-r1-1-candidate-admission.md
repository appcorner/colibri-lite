# M6.3-R1.1 Deterministic Candidate-Admission Study

## Result

**`no_candidate_admitted`**. The two newly considered formats are Layer-0
safe-Rust symmetric INT8 with F32 per-output-row/input-group scales at group
sizes 64 and 32. The permanently stopped
`cpu-safe-rust-int8-group128-layer0-v1` was explicitly excluded and was not
reused, reopened, or used as a default.

## What was measured

The study validated the versioned selection record, frozen reference identity,
source hash, model revision, Apache-2.0 license, deterministic selection
algorithm, pre-registered characterization/held-out split, logit cap, and
failure-mode validator tests. The canonical read-only Layer-0 shard was
verified before conversion.

Both new layouts were converted only into unique flat temporary run directories
after preflight. Group-64 produced `641728512` bytes with SHA-256
`35a3ef6aba723d302fb1a7fded6ede4543a0c3dfcd35651596b78f1c5158cad2`;
its independent reconversion was byte-identical. Group-32 produced
`679477248` bytes with SHA-256
`777820df3bfd7fa918035757245611c033f80eda9c92bbfca577f61ef504b1a2`;
its independent reconversion was byte-identical. A narrow safe-Rust proof
shows direct packed-value/scale consumption with zero complete F32 projection
or expert materializations. No payload is retained or committed.

## What was not measured

No Layer-0 routed-expert characterization execution, checkpoint divergence,
router result, or justified logit envelope exists. Artifact hashes and narrow
direct-consumption proof cannot replace those gates, so both candidates are
rejected. No held-out English/Thai final comparison, token gate, throughput,
memory, or I/O comparison was run; R1.2 was not started.

## Locked numerical gates

Before any final fixture execution, the record fixes structural/hash/offset/
shape/dtype/finite checks; byte-identical reconversion; repeated deterministic
execution; exact safe-margin router IDs with near ties classified only as
ambiguous; and Layer-0 first-divergence localization. It reserves exact
top-20 IDs, greedy IDs, and multi-token outputs as held-out R1.2 gates.

The candidate logit envelope must be justified from the pre-registered
characterization set and be at most `0.05`, below one quarter of the minimum
frozen held-out top-1 margin (`0.20189857482910156`). Delta-NLL, top-k overlap,
and endpoint agreement cannot excuse a failed gate. The validator rejects a
post-hoc threshold change, malformed provenance/hashes, nondeterminism,
whole-projection/expert F32 expansion, and more than one admitted candidate.

## Scope boundary

`no_candidate_admitted` does not authorize M6.3-R1.2, R1.3, R1.4, or M6.4.
R1.2 remains blocked pending a newly admitted candidate from a separately
available canonical conversion input.
