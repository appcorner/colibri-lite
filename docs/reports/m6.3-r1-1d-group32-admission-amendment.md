# M6.3-R1.1d Group-32 Admission Amendment Review

## Outcome

**GO for R1.2 only.** `cpu-safe-rust-int8-group32-layer0-r1-1a` is prospectively admitted as the single R1.2 candidate. The completed R1.1 `no_candidate_admitted` result is preserved byte-for-byte and remains historical evidence.

## Why the amendment is justified

The original R1.1 rejection identified missing characterization-stage evidence rather than a demonstrated numerical failure. R1.1a subsequently supplied that missing evidence under pre-registered contracts: three valid deterministic `code_newline` runs, exact Layer-0 router IDs, finite named checkpoints, first divergence at `layer0.selected_expert_output`, direct packed-value/scale consumption, zero complete F32 weight materializations, and nonzero process-correlated physical-I/O evidence with zero lost events.

Group-32's three valid runs were bit-identical on deterministic measurements. Its fixed-logit maximum absolute error was `2.36730575561523438e-2`, below the unchanged pre-registered `0.05` cap. ADR 0068 selected group-32 over group-64 using the locked ADR 0059 first ranking criterion.

## Admission boundary

This review does not reinterpret R1.1a as a retroactive R1.1 pass. Instead ADR 0069 and `m6.3-r1-1d-admission-amendment-v1.json` create a new prospective decision layer. The old R1.1 record remains `no_candidate_admitted`; the amendment alone authorizes group-32 to proceed to R1.2.

The admitted scope is Layer-0 validation only. R1.2 must use the held-out `short_english` and `short_thai` fixtures, preserve `reference-f32-v1`, and execute the existing stage-level, exact safe-margin router, fixed top-20, greedy, multi-token, and repeatability checks. Candidate reselection is prohibited.

## Still blocked

- R1.3 paired resource/throughput measurement remains blocked until R1.2 passes.
- R1.4 re-entry review remains pending.
- M6.4 all-layer implementation remains blocked and requires a separate all-layer plan review even if the Layer-0 slice passes.
- Group-64 is not a fallback candidate.
- The stopped group-128 candidate remains excluded.
