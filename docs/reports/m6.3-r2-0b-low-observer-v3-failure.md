The Thai reference +13.2034% and Thai candidate +23.0779% outliers fail the
unchanged every-pair maximum of 10%. Large negative values in other pairs show
that the same block-level estimator is also susceptible to favorable noise.

## Decision

- R2.0c remains blocked; localization sample count remains zero.
- v3 controls remain failed evidence and are never retried/reclassified.
- The 5% median and 10% every-pair numerical limits are not relaxed.
- A new preregistered observer method may split the same 25 timed iterations
  into matched interleaved mini-pairs and use a robust within-process estimator.
- Model arithmetic, F32 coarse scopes, candidate timing, fixtures, localization
  matrix, and classification thresholds remain unchanged.
